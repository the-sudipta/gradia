# Support Gradia and its Research Program

Gradia is being developed as both a practical teacher tool and a foundation for
research into trustworthy, human-centered academic software.

## What support enables

- usability studies with diverse teachers and institutions;
- accessibility, security, and privacy audits;
- signed Windows, macOS, and Linux distribution;
- encrypted, recoverable backup research;
- reproducible learning-analytics validation;
- institutional pilot support and documentation;
- conference, publication, and doctoral research costs; and
- sustained engineering, testing, and maintenance.

## Collaboration sought

The maintainer welcomes:

- industrial PhD positions abroad;
- funded doctoral scholarships and university supervision;
- education-technology research partnerships;
- responsible institutional pilots;
- grants, sponsorships, equipment, cloud-testing resources, and travel support; and
- commercial licensing conversations.

Read the [Industrial PhD & Research Collaboration Prospectus](docs/INDUSTRIAL_PHD_PROSPECTUS.md)
for the research case and proposed directions.
