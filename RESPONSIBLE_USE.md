# Responsible Use Policy

Gradia is intended to support lawful, ethical, accurate, and privacy-respecting
academic assessment.

## Required use

Users are responsible for:

- having authority to process each student record and institutional workbook;
- following applicable privacy, education, employment, records-retention, and
  accessibility requirements;
- checking grade policies, calculations, conversions, and exports before relying on
  them;
- keeping the local database and `.gradia` backups in protected storage;
- limiting access to people with a legitimate academic need; and
- maintaining independent institutional records and recovery procedures where
  required.

## Forbidden use

Do not use Gradia to:

- alter marks, attendance, identities, or records without authorization;
- facilitate cheating, credential fraud, discriminatory grading, retaliation, or
  academic misconduct;
- conceal required records, audit evidence, or conflicts of interest;
- surveil, profile, or rank people unlawfully or without a legitimate educational
  purpose;
- expose student data through screenshots, logs, issue reports, public repositories,
  or unprotected backups;
- violate sanctions, export restrictions, intellectual-property rights, contracts,
  institutional rules, or other applicable obligations; or
- manage any operation whose primary purpose is illegal or harmful.

## Human responsibility

Gradia’s analytics are descriptive. They do not establish causation and must not be
used as the sole basis for high-impact decisions about a student. A qualified human
must review consequential decisions, boundary cases, missing data, accommodations,
and exceptional circumstances.

## Reporting misuse

Report project-related abuse through a private contact route on the maintainer’s
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/the-sudipta). Do not attach real student data.
If immediate danger or a legal reporting duty exists, contact the appropriate local
authority or institutional officer.
