# Governance

Gradia is an owner-led source-available project.

## Maintainer

Sudipta Kumar (`the-sudipta`) is the project owner and final maintainer. The
maintainer controls releases, roadmap priorities, repository access, branding,
security disclosure, and commercial licensing.

## Decisions

Decisions consider:

1. student and teacher safety;
2. correctness and auditability;
3. user value and workflow simplicity;
4. privacy and local-first operation;
5. institutional interoperability;
6. maintainability and verification cost; and
7. alignment with the published product mission.

Substantial decisions should be recorded in an issue, pull request, project memory,
or architecture note with the evidence and trade-offs.

## Contributions

Contributions are reviewed under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
contribution clause in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Acceptance does not create employment,
partnership, joint ownership, or a right to a commercial license.

## Releases

A release requires passing automated checks, accurate release notes, synchronized
version metadata, reviewed artifacts, checksums, and a privacy audit. The maintainer
may delay a release when evidence is incomplete.

## Commercial agreements

Only the project owner or an expressly authorized representative may negotiate or
sign a Gradia commercial license. Community participation cannot grant commercial
rights.
