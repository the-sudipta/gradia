# Gradia Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Gradia welcomes teachers, students, researchers, engineers, designers, quality
professionals, institutions, and responsible commercial partners. Participation
must be respectful, evidence-based, privacy-conscious, and focused on improving
academic work.

Expected behavior includes:

- treating people with dignity regardless of background, identity, ability,
  experience, institution, or viewpoint;
- offering specific, constructive feedback about work rather than attacking people;
- acknowledging uncertainty, mistakes, sources, and contributions;
- protecting student, teacher, and institutional data;
- respecting academic integrity and legitimate institutional policy;
- accepting maintainer decisions without harassment; and
- giving collaborators reasonable space and time to respond.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, hate speech, stalking, sexualized attention, or intimidation;
- publishing private information or student records without explicit authority;
- fabricated bug evidence, research claims, identities, citations, or credentials;
- encouraging grade manipulation, unauthorized record changes, cheating, fraud, or
  other illegal conduct;
- repeated disruption, spam, coercive commercial solicitation, or retaliation; and
- using project communication channels to shame students, teachers, institutions, or
  contributors.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions,
project events, and public spaces where someone represents Gradia. Product-use
restrictions are addressed separately in [RESPONSIBLE_USE.md](RESPONSIBLE_USE.md)
and [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close participation, reject
contributions, temporarily restrict interaction, or permanently ban a participant
when reasonably necessary to protect the community or project.

Report a concern privately through a contact method on the maintainer’s
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/the-sudipta). Do not include student data,
credentials, or unnecessary personal information. Reports will be reviewed in good
faith, with confidentiality respected as far as practical and lawful.

## Good-faith interpretation

Context, severity, repetition, impact, corrective action, and safety all matter.
This code is a community standard, not a substitute for institutional policy,
employment procedure, or law.
