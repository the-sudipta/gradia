# Gradia Roadmap

The roadmap is evidence-driven. An item is not considered delivered until code,
tests, documentation, and user-facing verification agree.

## 0.2 — Faster daily work

- multi-cell paste, fill-down, and safer bulk editing;
- visible per-entry history and targeted undo;
- attendance bulk actions and keyboard status shortcuts;
- CSV roster import alongside XLSX;
- field rename, reorder, archive, and reusable view management; and
- larger 40-student × 20-field interactive performance validation.

## 0.3 — Deeper academic intelligence

- course, section, and semester comparisons;
- time trends and assessment-component trajectories;
- scatter plots, correlation views, box plots, and robust outlier review;
- OBE/CLO/PLO mapping and radar summaries;
- configurable intervention indicators with transparent thresholds; and
- exportable, reproducible analysis reports.

## 0.4 — Portability and institutional readiness

- encrypted backup containers and managed key-recovery guidance;
- signed/notarized macOS package and tested migration path;
- packaged Linux releases across selected distributions;
- accessibility audit and full keyboard/screen-reader remediation;
- clean-profile installer and upgrade tests; and
- documented institutional deployment and retention controls.

## Research track

- trustworthy end-user programming for assessment rules;
- provenance and explainability for spreadsheet-to-database migration;
- privacy-preserving analytics for small sections;
- human factors in exception-first attendance and focused mark entry;
- invariant-based fidelity verification for Office OpenXML transformations; and
- responsible decision support that separates observation from causation.
